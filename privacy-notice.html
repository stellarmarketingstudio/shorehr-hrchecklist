What this notice covers

This notice covers two online tools: the HR Health Check and the Workplace Audit. It explains what happens if you fill in the short form at the end of either one. It does not cover the main Shore HR website, which has its own privacy policy.

You can use the tools without giving us anything

You can answer the questions and see your full results without giving us any personal information at all. We only receive your details if you choose to fill in the optional form at the end and submit it.

What we collect

If you submit the form, we collect:

  • your first name
  • your email address
  • your business or organisation name (optional)
  • your answers to the seven questions, your score, and your result (Green, Amber or Red)

Why we collect it, and our lawful basis

We use your information for two things, and both rely on your consent:

  • To review your answers and follow up. Natalie looks at your answers personally and gets in touch about your results. This is the consent you give when you tick the box to submit the form.
  • To keep in touch. If you give separate permission, we may send you occasional HR tips and updates we think you will find useful.

You can withdraw either consent at any time. See "Your rights" below.

Who we share it with

Your form submission is delivered to us by Formspree, the service that handles the form. Formspree acts as our data processor and is based in the United States, so your information is transferred outside the UK. That transfer is covered by appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your information until you ask us to remove it. When you ask, we delete it, unless we have to keep something to meet a legal obligation. If you go on to become a Shore HR client, your information will then be handled under our client terms.

Cookies

These tools do not use cookies or any tracking. Nothing is stored on your device.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, to ask us to correct it, to ask us to delete it, to ask us to restrict or stop how we use it, to object to how we use it, to ask us to transfer it, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

To do any of these, email us at hello@shorehr.co.uk. We will respond within one month.

Every "keep in touch" email also has an unsubscribe link, so you can stop those at any time in one click.

If you are not happy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. If we do, we will change the "last updated" date at the top of this page.
